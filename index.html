Vishwajeet Hogale

Hello! I'm Vishwajeet, an MS student in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University's Khoury College, specializing in Machine Learning and graduating in December 2026. I spent 2025 and 2026 interning at Robert Bosch LLC in Sunnyvale. As an Autonomous Driving ML Intern, I worked on scene-tagging and scenario-mining pipelines for driving data, and on the AI Forensics team I owned evaluation for a multi-agent vision-language triage platform. I completed my undergraduate degree at PES University, receiving a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science with a focus on Machine Intelligence and Data Science, and then spent two years as a Data Analyst at Mercedes-Benz R&D India.

My work spans multimodal learning, vision-language models, and self-supervised representation learning, and I've also explored LiDAR-based 3D perception, agentic systems, and medical image captioning. At Northeastern, I'm a Teaching Assistant for CS 7150 Deep Learning with Prof. David Bau, and I previously TA'd DS 4420 Machine Learning and Data Mining 2 with Prof. Eric Gerber and DS 4440 Practical Neural Networks with Prof. Steve Schmidt.

I enjoy building things end to end and seeing how well they hold up in the real world. Right now, that happens to be in autonomous driving!

Research Interests: Multimodal Learning, Self-Supervised Representation Learning, Agentic Systems for Autonomous Driving
